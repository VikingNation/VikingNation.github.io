## How we got here

There have been many twists and turns in the ongoing VARA saga:

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| October 2026 | A masked developer, x0rro, used AI to build and release XARA, an open-source VARA-compatible modem. ([post]({% post_url 2026-10-31-xara-modem-Windows %})) |
| November 8 | x0rro shut down XARA and removed the source code. ([post]({% post_url 2026-11-08-xara-project-shuttered %})) |
| November 4–11 | VARA HF 4.9.1 and FM 4.4.1 were set to break compatibility with older versions on January 1, 2027. ([post]({% post_url 2026-11-11-vara-breaks-compatibility-2027 %})) |
| November 19 | The VARA developer reversed course with HF 4.9.2 and FM 4.4.2, but those releases had a bug. ([post]({% post_url 2026-11-19-vara-compatibility-restored-macos-linux %})) |
| November 20 | HF 4.9.3 and FM 4.4.3 fixed the bug. ([post]({% post_url 2026-11-20-vara-hf-493-fm-443-release %})) |
| **November 21** | **"No More Secrets" released the full VARA modem specification.** |
