## What happened

On November 19, EA5HVK, the developer of VARA, released VARA HF v4.9.2 and VARA FM v4.4.2. These versions were supposed to restore compatibility with older versions of VARA, as I covered in [yesterday's post]({% post_url 2026-11-19-vara-compatibility-restored-macos-linux %}). When operators tested them, they could not connect to stations running older versions.

A day later, on November 20, VARA HF v4.9.3 and VARA FM v4.4.3 were released. The developer's page, [rosmodem.wordpress.com](https://rosmodem.wordpress.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, now says:

> **ATTENTION!**
> A new versions just released, compatible with the older versions. Replace VARA HF v4.9.1 by v4.9.3 and VARA FM v4.4.1 by v4.4.3.

| Product | Replace | With |
|---|---|---|
| VARA HF | v4.9.1 or v4.9.2 | **v4.9.3** |
| VARA FM | v4.4.1 or v4.4.2 | **v4.4.3** |

The same page still announces the work on native macOS and Linux versions of VARA for Winlink and VarAC.
